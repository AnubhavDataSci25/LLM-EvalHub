## Leave

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of annual leave per calendar year, accruing at
1.667 days per completed month of service. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into
the next year and must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited.

Sick leave is 10 paid days per year. A medical certificate is required from the
fourth consecutive day of absence. Unused sick leave does not carry over.

Parental leave is 18 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks at
full pay for the secondary caregiver. Eligibility begins after 12 months of
continuous service.

Bereavement leave is 5 days for an immediate family member and 2 days for an
extended family member.

## Working arrangements

The standard working week is 38 hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to
15:00 in the employee's local time zone; work outside those hours is flexible.

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week by default. Fully remote
arrangements require director approval and a signed remote-work agreement.

Requests to change working pattern must be submitted at least 30 days before the
intended start date.

## Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense being incurred.
Claims submitted after 60 days require VP approval and are paid at the company's
discretion.

Meal allowance while travelling is 45 USD per day domestically and 70 USD per day
internationally. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Home-office equipment is reimbursed up to 800 USD in the first year of employment
and up to 400 USD every two years thereafter.

## Performance and progression

Performance reviews run twice a year, in March and September. Promotion decisions
are made only in the September cycle.

Employees must complete 6 months of service before becoming eligible for
promotion. A performance improvement plan runs for 8 weeks and pauses promotion
eligibility for its duration.

## Exit

The notice period is 4 weeks for individual contributors and 8 weeks for managers
and above. Accrued but unused annual leave is paid out on the final payslip;
unused sick leave is not paid out.

Company equipment must be returned within 5 business days of the last working day.
